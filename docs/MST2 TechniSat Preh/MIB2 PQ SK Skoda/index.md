# MIB2 PQ SK Skoda

The last Skoda PQ SW is 25x and there is no 4xx available for PQ. However it is possible to flash the PQ 4xx like 478 oder 480 from VW as can be seen in various Articles. After that is is possible to copy the complete content of the Skoda ZR HMI 4xx tar.gz to get back the Skoda Design,BUT some features wont work. Most of the daily Features do work very well. As well known the CAR menu will not really be worth it, as most stuff will not work properly. But also the BAP connection to the Instrumentcluster wont work any more , so the AUDIO entry in the Instrumentluster will only show rubbish or most of the time only "Skoda Navigation". It is not possible to transfer the Skoda ZR Skin files to the VW HMI Binaries. In order to  get the  Instumentcluster AUDIO functionality  back , you will have to stick to a clean HMI installation.
